# DeutschIQ free soft launch

## Purpose

Validate learning clarity, usefulness, retention and willingness to pay without charging anyone. The release remains invite-only, `BETA_FREE_ACCESS=true`, and `PAYMENTS_ENABLED=false`.

## Small-cohort route

DeutschIQ does not require 20 testers before continuing. Start with 3–5 genuine learners and add more gradually. One person completing the full journey is more valuable than many people opening the app once.

1. Create single-use links in the owner Quality Center.
2. Ask each learner to complete onboarding, the placement test and at least one lesson.
3. Let the app record starts, completions, abandonments, returns, errors and Pro-interest clicks.
4. Ask learners to use the feedback button on the screen where confusion occurs.
5. Review anonymised tester progress, feedback and evidence gates in the Quality Center.

## Feedback questions

Profile collects three short answers in Russian, German or English:

1. What was confusing?
2. Was DeutschIQ useful? — Yes / Partly / No
3. Would you buy Pro for 700 Telegram Stars? — Yes / Maybe / No

The floating contextual reporter also records the current page and, inside lessons, the lesson/exercise context.

## Payment evidence gate

Payments remain disabled until the owner dashboard confirms all five conditions:

- 10 completed diagnostics
- 5 completed lessons
- 3 learners returning on another day
- 2 genuine Pro-interest signals
- zero serious production error signals in the selected period

Meeting these numbers is evidence to review, not automatic authorization to launch. Legal, support and payment-flow checks must also be complete.
